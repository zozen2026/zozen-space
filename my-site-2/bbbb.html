English

Instant Access on Correct Passkey: When another device enters the correct account passkey, it logs into the account immediately without sending you a verification message to approve the login from another device (it logs in directly upon entering the correct passkey).

Approval Required Before Access: When another device enters the correct account passkey, a prompt will be sent to you asking for your approval to allow the other device to access the account after entering the correct passkey (you can decline).

Block Account Access: Even if the account credentials are entered correctly, access to the account will never be granted.
